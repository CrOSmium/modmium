## I'm unenrolled, why bother with Modmium?
Modmium is designed to give users the freedoms of an unmanaged device without requiring them to unenroll.

This is especially useful for:
* People who need their Kiosk (Sign out) apps during PSAT or state testing season.
* People who want to stay visible to their district's monitoring extension.
* People who want fewer restrictions but keep their managed account data.

## I want to use Modmium unverified.
This isn't recommended, but we can't stop you. Enrolling while being unverified can trigger alerts in your district's Google Admin Console (GAC), potentially resulting in your device being locked.
